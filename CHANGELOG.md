# Changelog

## 0.15.0

- A chat tab of its own, so the extension runs without any other extension.
- Chats and projects in one section, filtered to the selected account, with a switch for all accounts.
- Usage simplified: two limits up front, history and models one click away. Weekly or monthly history, one frame at a time, with a smooth line, zoom and session hover.
- Usage refreshes by itself while the panel is open, and instantly when you pick another account.
- A bell shows how many sessions still fit before the weekly reset.
- Account pictures use one calm style, with more letters when initials clash.
- Clearer notifications and settings text.
- Models tab lists every model you have used, each once.
- A quiet, rare invitation to rate the extension.
- Screenshots and animations in the README.
- Session windows from now to the weekly reset, with times on hover, and an optional one-shot auto-start.
- The bell card folds to one line, auto-starts show in the status bar, and two test commands let you try the alert and the scheduler.
- The bell card tells you how much of each session to use to finish the week, with a gauge per session window.
- Every session, weekly sample and the models each session used are kept for three years, and can be exported as JSON or CSV.
- One accent colour (your theme's focus colour) across the panel and chat tab, and a tidier type scale.
- The session timeline stays small with many windows: thinner boxes, fewer time labels, day marks.
- Weekly reminder notifications no longer repeat when usage refreshes (a reset on a half hour, like 11:30, used to look like a new week every time), and have a Mute Reminders button.
- Weekly reminder notifications are rare and never repeat on refresh; auto-start sends from one window only and the new session shows right away.
- Usage refreshes every 30 seconds while the panel is open (setting: refreshSeconds).
