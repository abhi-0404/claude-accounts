# Changelog

## 0.15.0

- A chat tab of its own, so the extension runs without any other extension.
- Chats and projects in one section, filtered to the selected account, with a switch for all accounts.
- Usage simplified: two limits up front, history and models one click away. Weekly or monthly history, one frame at a time, with a smooth line, zoom and session hover.
- Usage refreshes by itself while the panel is open, and instantly when you pick another account.
- A bell shows how many sessions still fit before the weekly reset.
- Account pictures use one calm style, with more letters when initials clash.
- Clearer notifications and settings text.
- Models tab lists every model you have used, each once.
- A quiet, rare invitation to rate the extension.
- Screenshots and animations in the README.
