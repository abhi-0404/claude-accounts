Weekly alert and auto-start: try it in the browser

This page plays what the extension does, with made-up accounts. Nothing here uses your real usage, and no message is sent.

1
Show the alertLike the command "Test: Simulate Weekly Alert": a notification, the bell, and the session windows card.
2
Look at the cardHover a window for its times. Click the card's title line to fold it. Click a later window to turn its auto-start on or off.
3
Watch the status barA clock appears at the bottom right while an auto-start is set. Click it to see or cancel.
4
Skip the waitRun the next auto-start right now to see what happens when its time comes.
5
Many sessionsSee how the card stays small as the number of sessions until the reset grows. Try a narrow panel too.
